The app

One gesture. No menus.

Pick a photo and throw it toward the laptop. Everything else stays out of the way.

  • Pairs over the network — no QR codes
  • Reconnects by itself
  • Keeps working in the background

Set up once.

  1. Install the receiver on your Linux laptop with ./install.sh, then log out and back in.
  2. Pair by scanning the code on the laptop with the phone app. Both sides pin each other's certificate.
  3. Flick. That's it. It reconnects by itself from then on.

Questions.

What do I need?

An Android phone and a Linux laptop running GNOME (Wayland), on the same network or connected by USB.

Is it secure?

Devices pair once over your local network (you accept on the laptop and confirm a 6-digit code) and talk over mutually authenticated TLS. Files go directly between your devices.

Is it free?

The Linux receiver and GNOME extension are free and open source.

Windows? iPhone?

Windows support is planned. Today it is Android + Linux.

Where do files go?

On the laptop, to Downloads/DropTouch or your Desktop. On the phone, to Pictures/DropTouch or Download/DropTouch.
